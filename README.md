# Privacy Policy for KDJ Singlish Keyboard

**Effective date:** October 3, 2026  
**Developer:** KDJ Lanka (Pvt) Ltd  
**Contact:** hello@kdj.lk

KDJ Singlish Keyboard is an Android keyboard for English and Sinhala typing. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

## Typing and local features

The keyboard processes the text you type on your device to enter characters, convert Singlish to Sinhala, show suggestions, and apply text shortcuts. The developer does not receive your typed text through a KDJ server. The keyboard may save learned words, shortcuts, preferences, and clipboard history in the app's local storage to provide these features. Password fields are excluded from word learning. You can turn on Incognito mode to stop saving new learned words and clips.

The clipboard feature reads clipboard content when you use it so you can view and paste copied text. Clipboard content is handled on your device. You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Voice input

Voice input is optional and requires microphone permission. When you start voice input, the app uses the speech recognition service available on your Android device. That service may process audio according to its own privacy policy and device settings. KDJ Lanka (Pvt) Ltd does not receive or store the audio or the recognized speech on a KDJ server.

## Data sharing and security

The developer does not use this keyboard to send typed text, clipboard content, learned words, or shortcuts to a KDJ server, and does not sell this information. Local app data is stored in Android's app storage, which is protected by the device's access controls. Information you choose to enter into another app is handled by that app under its own policies.

## Retention and deletion

Locally saved settings, shortcuts, learned words, and clipboard history remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. The developer does not keep a server copy to delete.

## Changes and questions

We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. For questions or privacy requests, contact **hello@kdj.lk**.
